ABOUT ME

HI, I’M MOHAN IRAYYA

I’m Mohan Irayya Naik, a passionate wildlife and nature photographer from Karnataka, India. My photography journey is driven by a deep love for nature and a curiosity to explore the beauty of the natural world.

I specialize in capturing birds, wildlife, macro subjects, and landscapes, focusing on the beauty, behaviour, and unique moments of nature. Through my lens, I aim to tell meaningful visual stories and create images that inspire people to appreciate and protect nature.

Photography is not just my passion; it’s a journey of exploration, patience, and storytelling.
